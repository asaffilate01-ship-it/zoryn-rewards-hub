# Legal drafting headings — professional review required

Consumer terms: eligibility, earning, pending rewards, redemption, expiry, reversals, misuse, suspension, complaints.

Merchant agreement: funding, fees, campaigns, settlement, data processing, security, suspension and audit.

Privacy notice: purposes, legal bases, processors, retention, profiling, rights and supervisory authority.

Affiliate disclosure: tracking, commercial relationship, pending/approved/reversed rewards and exclusions.
